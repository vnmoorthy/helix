Every viral video
has a genome.

Helix reads what short-form videos show, say, overlay and sound like, measures which of those genes actually lift views in your niche, and forks the winning structure into a shot list you can film on Monday.

Shows

AI-Vision probes find face-cam, hands-on-product, screen recordings, before/after — at the exact frame.

Says

Timestamped transcripts expose the hook: questions, listicles, contrarian openers, time-to-first-word, pace.

Overlays

On-screen text regions with polygons: text hooks in frame one, burned-in captions, ALL-CAPS, POV lines.

Sounds like

Original audio vs licensed tracks, and which sounds are trending across the niche right now.

1 · Sequence

Pull the hits and the long tail of a niche from Oriane — found by what's said, overlaid and captioned, then probed by AI Vision — and extract 32 genes per video, each with a timestamp and source field.

2 · Measure lift

For every gene: median views-per-follower with vs. without it, and its share of top- vs. bottom-quartile videos. The winning genome is what the data says, not what a guru says.

3 · Fork

Inherit the structure, never the content. Helix turns the winning genome into a 4-beat shot list for your product — or batch-forks it across your whole creator roster.
