# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — Hack the Feed (Nov 6 2026)

Built in one afternoon at **Hack the Feed** (Amplitude HQ, SF), on top of **Oriane** video intelligence.

- **Sequence a niche.** Oriane's named sub-queries (said · on-screen · caption) pull the hits and the long tail, with full frames, transcripts and OCR.
- **32 genes across 5 tracks.** Visual (6 AI-Vision probes), Spoken, On-screen, Sound and Format. Every gene comes with evidence, its source field and a timestamp.
- **Lift statistics.** Median views-per-follower with vs. without each gene, top-vs-bottom-quartile share, and the winning genome.
- **5-track video genome.** Frames, transcript timeline, OCR polygons, sound and format.
- **Fork.** A 4-beat shot list that keeps the structure and replaces the content, with an agency roster batch fork and Markdown/print export.
- **Live data bundled.** Real Oriane sequences for `protein coffee`, `ai tools`, `skincare routine`, `home workout` and `meal prep`.
- **Pitch deck.** 10 slides as motion HTML and PPTX, plus a 3-minute storyboard.
- **Real finding.** In `protein coffee`, 84% of videos never mention the niche in their caption. 30–60s videos get 7.2× the views per follower, and licensed music 5.7×.
